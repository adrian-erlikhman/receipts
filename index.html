    After a check, each matched sentence gets an “Ask Claude” button. Claude reads the sentence and the passage and says whether it supports it, partly supports it, or doesn't. Its answer has to quote the passage word for word or it is thrown out. Your key stays in this tab and goes only to api.anthropic.com.

    How it works

    Match

    Each sentence of your essay is compared with every passage of your sources by the words and word pairs they share, weighted by how rare those words are (TF-IDF). Turn on the on-device model and it also compares meaning, so a real paraphrase still finds its source.

    Check

    Quotations must appear word for word in a source. Seven or more words in a row from a source without quotation marks is flagged. Figures are compared with the same kind of figure in the source (“43 minutes” against “34 minutes”). Citations must point to the source the sentence actually uses.

    Fix

    Click any sentence to see the passage it matched, with the shared words marked, and what to change. Sentences with no match are left alone: that's your own argument.

    Receipts helps you check your own work. It isn't a plagiarism detector for other people's work, and its matches are suggestions to review, not verdicts.
